# Synopsis pre-pass prompt   [rubric-r2: per-doc synopsis, Syed's context decision 2026-07-05]

You will be shown the beginning of a long multi-part document (usually a forum thread). Write
a synopsis of AT MOST 150 words for a judge who will later read the document in chunks:

- What the document is (thread subject, platform/vehicle, the author's goal).
- The running context a mid-document reader needs: ongoing experiments/builds, key hardware,
  what "the current setup" refers to.
- Factual only. No quality assessment, no score hints, no praise or criticism, the judge
  must not be biased by your framing.

Reply with ONLY the synopsis text.
